Experiments

One question each, asked directly. A lab may be ugly, may be wrong, and may be deleted once it has answered what it was built to ask.

Labs are not chapters. A chapter explains something settled; a lab is where the finding out happens. Nothing in src/ depends on one, and a lab graduates into the story only when its answer is worth telling. See plan.md §7.1.

  • The packings, with numbers answered Which packings are there, and what are they like? Every integral packing has exactly one root quadruple and the conditions are decidable, so they enumerate — and can then be measured and searched. Which have an 8 in them, which have a repeated bend, which are riddled with holes. It found that 17 of the first 96 roots could not be placed at all, which is now fixed.
  • Designing a quadruple a tool What is a designer actually working inside? Nudge four bends and watch every constraint at once: whether Descartes holds, both completions, the common factor, the residues mod 24, which root it belongs to and how many Vieta steps away it is. Hover for a circle's exact bend and centre. Less a question than the answer to one — the workbench's custom field rejected you after the fact, and this shows you the rules as you move.
  • Turning a packing inside out open The Vieta recursion only goes inward. What is on the other side? Reflect a quadruple in one of its own circles and you go outward, through the strip and beyond — Jake's construction. It is an involution, it is not the Vieta jump, it is exact, and the Schmidt arrangement is closed under it. Still open: whether this is the dual Apollonian group, which needs the paper rather than a script.
  • Schmidt subdivision answered What does one generation of the subdivision look like? Drawing every region's circumscribed circle at once turned out to superimpose boundaries with circumscribed circles — two different things — and was unreadable. Drawing the partition instead is legible, and that finding became chapter 6.
  • Where the gasket sits graduated Does every circle of the doubled packing appear in the arrangement, and how deep must it go? Answered: all 69 with bend ≤ 100 are there, and the ones that go missing are where the arrangement has not reached rather than circles it lacks. It is chapter 7 now.
  • Exact against floating point withdrawn The plan was a packing computed both ways, zoomed until the float version's tangencies visibly came apart. They do not. The recursion is addition and subtraction, so doubles reproduce the integers exactly for 33 steps and then fail at 2⁵³ — silently, still returning whole numbers. There is no picture to draw, and the real finding is in chapter 3 instead.
