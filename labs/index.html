One question each, asked directly. A lab may be ugly, may be wrong, and may be deleted once it has answered what it was built to ask.

Labs are not chapters. A chapter explains something settled; a lab is where the finding out happens. Nothing in src/ depends on one, and a lab graduates into the story only when its answer is worth telling. See plan.md §7.1.

  • Schmidt subdivision open What does one generation of the subdivision look like, and do the two region types behave differently as it deepens? Drawing every region's circumscribed circle at once turned out to superimpose boundaries and circumscribed circles — two different things — so this now draws the partition instead.
  • Where the gasket sits The arrangement contains (−2, 4, 4, 6) — the classic packing doubled. Does every circle of that packing appear, and how deep must the arrangement go to find them? Blocked on pruning the region walk rather than only its output.
  • Exact against floating point The same packing computed both ways, zoomed until the float version's tangencies visibly come apart. Intended for chapter 3.
