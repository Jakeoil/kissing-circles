Kissing Circles

A story about circles that touch

Four circles, each touching the other three. That constraint is tight enough to force the numbers to be whole, and loose enough to fill a plane. This is where that goes.

Every figure here is live and computed in exact integer arithmetic — the same code as the workbench, which is where to go once you would rather poke at something than read about it.
